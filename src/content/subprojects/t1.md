The blocks of the kit are described in machine-readable form, and a virtual demonstrator of the process is built from these descriptions.
Its coupling to the real system is being implemented.

Because single-point incremental forming proved unworkable as a second stage after stamp forming, T1 moved to post-forming with the actuators of the gripper frame.
There, higher gripping forces reduced spring-in, depending on the fibre orientation.
